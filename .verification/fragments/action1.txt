## What to build

Establish the family-neutral append-only Evidence correction primitives and the observation-specific correction path: immutable `EvidenceCorrectionId` acts, exact same-family ancestry, complete observation `REVISE` replacement assertions, `RETRACT`, deterministic recursive restoration, sibling support, contestation, and historical inspection without recency-based winner selection. Binding and sufficiency-assessment correction paths are deliberately excluded and move to their own DD-2 reslice destinations.

## Acceptance criteria

* [ ] Every observation correction is an immutable `EvidenceCorrectionId` act targeting exactly one `EvidenceObservationId` root or earlier observation correction from that same root; ancestry is acyclic and the family-neutral correction-reference primitives are reusable by the later binding and assessment slices without weakening same-family typing.
* [ ] Observation `REVISE` carries the complete replacement assertion for correction of the record of the same acquisition/observation act only; later acquisition, a newly published source version, or materially different observed content creates a new `EvidenceObservationId` root.
* [ ] `RETRACT` withdraws its target without inventing `RESTORE`, `CONTEST`, `SUPERSEDE`, `QUALIFY`, or `DISCONFIRM` Evidence correction effects; retraction recursively restores the branch interpretation immediately preceding the retracted correction.
* [ ] Sibling branches remain independent; materially equivalent surviving assertions coalesce and union complete support; incompatible positive branches or positive-versus-withdrawal support produce `CONTESTED`.
* [ ] Recency, UUID ordering, database ordering, and latest-write-wins never choose truth; missing or unknown ancestry is invalid or incomplete history rather than contestation.
* [ ] Persistence proves append-only observation correction history, exact ancestry/reference validity, rollback, restart reconstruction, idempotent exact replay, and rejection of changed semantic `OperationId` reuse behind the inward-owned greenfield persistence boundary.
* [ ] Binding `REVISE`/`RETRACT` and sufficiency-assessment `REVISE`/`RETRACT`, including their family-specific replacement assertions and assessment trusted-commit semantics, are not implemented under this ticket.
