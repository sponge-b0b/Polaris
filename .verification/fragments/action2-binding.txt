## What to build

Implement the binding-specific `REVISE | RETRACT` path on the shared correction foundation established by #378. A binding correction remains within one `EvidenceBindingId` lineage only while the root's observation, typed target judgment, scope, and `EvidenceUse` stay fixed. `REVISE` carries the complete binding assertion, including role, material-use, availability, freshness basis/result when applicable, and material qualification; changing a fixed endpoint retracts the old binding and creates a new binding root rather than revising lineage.

## Acceptance criteria

* [ ] Each binding correction is an immutable `EvidenceCorrectionId` targeting exactly one `EvidenceBindingId` root or earlier binding correction from that same root; ancestry is acyclic and uses the shared family-neutral correction-reference primitives from #378.
* [ ] Binding `REVISE` carries the complete binding replacement assertion, including the fixed observation/typed target/scope/use coordinates plus role, materially-used state, availability, freshness requirement/basis/result when applicable, and material qualification.
* [ ] Changing the fixed observation, typed target judgment, scope, or `EvidenceUse` is rejected as revision of the existing binding lineage; the old binding is retracted and a distinct binding root is created instead.
* [ ] `RETRACT` and correction-of-correction recursively restore the immediately preceding branch interpretation; sibling branches remain independent, materially equivalent assertions coalesce with complete support, and incompatible surviving support yields `CONTESTED` without recency/UUID/database ordering.
* [ ] Material-use and availability/freshness invariants remain valid after every replacement interpretation; invalid or incomplete ancestry fails closed rather than being normalized into a winner.
* [ ] Persistence proves append-only binding correction history, ancestry/reference validity, rollback, restart reconstruction, exact replay idempotency, and greenfield inward-owned persistence constraints.
* [ ] Sufficiency-assessment correction, derived assessment proof, reassessment identity continuity, and trusted commit-time re-evaluation/revalidation are excluded from this ticket.
