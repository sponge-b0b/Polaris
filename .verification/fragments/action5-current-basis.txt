## What to build

Add current-support reads at exact `BasisScopeKey` granularity and return a `CurrentEvidenceBasis` capturing every positive version dependency and typed relied-upon absence needed by a later command, including the complete current interpreted observation-correction, binding-correction, and sufficiency-assessment/reassessment/correction state whenever it can affect the result or its support/provenance.

## Acceptance criteria

* [ ] `BasisScopeKey` is exactly `(InvestmentDecisionId, EvidenceJudgmentRef, scope, EvidenceUse)` and read time is explicit `T=K`.
* [ ] Basis contains the complete accepted Decision/target/claim/requirement/context/support-version dependency set plus the exact current interpreted observation and binding roots/correction branches used by the result and the exact current sufficiency assessment/reassessment/correction interpretation required by the result.
* [ ] Scope-local `EvidenceSupportVersion` starts at zero and advances exactly when current result or complete support/provenance changes, including a result-affecting observation correction/retraction/restoration/contestation, binding revision/retraction/restoration/contestation, or sufficiency assessment/reassessment/correction; time/query and irrelevant future-only or out-of-key state do not advance it.
* [ ] Negative predicates represent the exact accepted absence families, including absence/invalidity conditions whose meaning depends on the current interpreted observation, binding, or assessment state, and remain typed/scoped rather than generic strings.
* [ ] A result-affecting correction or succession of a referenced observation, a result-affecting revision/retraction/restoration/contestation of a relevant binding, or a result-affecting assessment/reassessment/correction invalidates the prior current basis even when unrelated endpoint/version fields still match; corrections outside the exact key do not invalidate unless they enter a protected dependency.
* [ ] Tests cover complete dependency capture, observation-correction invalidation, binding-correction invalidation, assessment correction/reassessment invalidation, restoration/contestation changes, support-only changes, irrelevant changes, broken absences, late-recorded history, claim/requirement/context invalidation, and key isolation.
