## What to build

Implement the append-only Evidence correction algebra for observation and binding roots, including typed correction identity/targets, complete `REVISE` replacement assertions, `RETRACT`, deterministic recursive restoration, sibling support, contestation, and historical inspection without recency-based winner selection. Sufficiency-assessment correction is deliberately excluded from this slice and is owned by the assessment-correction destination created by the DD-2 reconciliation.

## Acceptance criteria

* [ ] Every observation or binding correction is an immutable `EvidenceCorrectionId` act targeting exactly one same-family root or earlier correction; ancestry is acyclic.
* [ ] Observation `REVISE` remains correction of the same acquisition act only; later acquisition, a newly published source version, or materially different observed content creates a new `EvidenceObservationId` root rather than reusing correction identity.
* [ ] Binding `REVISE` preserves the root's fixed observation, typed target judgment, scope, and use; changing any fixed endpoint creates a new binding rather than revising the existing binding lineage.
* [ ] `REVISE` carries a complete family-specific replacement assertion; `RETRACT` withdraws its target without inventing `RESTORE`, `CONTEST`, `SUPERSEDE`, `QUALIFY`, or `DISCONFIRM` Evidence correction effects.
* [ ] Retraction recursively restores the immediately preceding branch interpretation; siblings remain independent; equivalent surviving assertions coalesce; incompatible positive branches or positive-versus-withdrawal support produce `CONTESTED`.
* [ ] Recency, UUID ordering, database ordering, and latest-write-wins never choose truth; missing or unknown ancestry is invalid or incomplete history rather than contestation.
* [ ] Persistence proves append-only observation/binding correction history, ancestry/reference validity, rollback, and restart reconstruction while preserving the inward-owned greenfield persistence boundary.
* [ ] Sufficiency-assessment `REVISE`/`RETRACT`, derived replacement proof, reassessment identity continuity, and trusted commit-time re-evaluation/revalidation are not implemented under this ticket.

Title: Correct sufficiency assessments with derived proof and trusted revalidation
Complete proposed body:

## Parent

Parent Spec: #357

## Spec obligations

## Architecture context

Evidence; Configuration; Application Use Cases; Durable Persistence; ADRs 0011, 0012, 0013, and 0014. All architecture and material design decisions currently required by this ticket are accepted; no known implementation-readiness blocker remains unresolved.

## Context fit

indivisible-two-window-exception — ADR 0014 requires an assessment-correction replacement to be Evidence-derived from the complete authoritative universe and freshly re-evaluated/revalidated at the trusted commit instant before the same append becomes authoritative; separating those two halves would leave an append path able to persist caller-authored or stale sufficiency proof.

## What to build

Add sufficiency-assessment `REVISE | RETRACT` as the assessment-specific extension of the shared Evidence correction algebra. A `REVISE` of a misrecorded assessment must derive its complete replacement disposition/proof through the Evidence-owned deterministic evaluator over the authoritative requirement set/version and complete interpreted binding universe; callers cannot author the authoritative disposition or select the authoritative support subset. Preserve same-root correction only for the same attributable assessment boundary and requirement-set/version contract. Reassessment caused by time, new Evidence, or changed requirements creates a new `EvidenceSufficiencyAssessmentId` root, with changed requirement version always creating a new reassessment root. At the trusted commit instant, freshly re-evaluate the replacement assessment and atomically revalidate the exact requirement assignment/version, complete binding and correction universe, support version, temporal freshness, and relied-upon negative predicates before append.

## Acceptance criteria

* [ ] Each assessment correction is an immutable `EvidenceCorrectionId` targeting exactly one `EvidenceSufficiencyAssessmentId` root or earlier assessment correction from that same root; ancestry is acyclic and uses the shared `REVISE | RETRACT` algebra.
* [ ] Assessment `REVISE` carries a complete replacement assertion whose disposition, contributors, deficiency witnesses, counted observations, freshness/no-freshness basis, negative witnesses, `EvidenceSupportVersion`, and absence guards are derived by Evidence from the complete exact-key interpreted binding universe; caller-authored authoritative results or caller-selected authoritative support subsets are rejected.
* [ ] A correction may retain the assessment root only for a misrecorded assertion at the same attributable assessment boundary and authoritative requirement set/version. Reassessment caused by time, new Evidence, or changed requirements creates a new assessment root; a changed requirement-set version may never be represented as correction of the existing assessment root.
* [ ] `RETRACT` withdraws its target and recursive interpretation restores the immediately preceding branch state; sibling branches remain independent, materially equivalent surviving assertions coalesce with complete support, and incompatible surviving support yields `CONTESTED` without recency/UUID/database ordering.
* [ ] At a fresh trusted commit instant, Evidence re-runs the deterministic assessment derivation at commit `T=K` and atomically revalidates the exact requirement version/assignment, complete binding and correction universe, support version, temporal freshness, and typed negative predicates before appending the correction. Matching stored versions alone are insufficient.
* [ ] Missing, unavailable, contested, incomplete, or invalid requirement authority; unavailable cross-owner revalidation; stale basis including time-only freshness change; invalid history; or persistence failure appends no assessment correction and remains typed/fail-closed.
* [ ] Historical assessment proof and prior corrections remain immutable and inspectable; exact replay is idempotent and changed semantic `OperationId` reuse is rejected.
* [ ] Domain/application tests and real PostgreSQL migration, round-trip, rollback, restart, and concurrency tests cover assessment revision/retraction/restoration/contestation, derived replacement proof, changed-requirement/new-root behavior, caller-authored proof rejection, commit-time re-evaluation, time-only invalidation, and greenfield inward-owned persistence constraints.
