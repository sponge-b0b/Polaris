# Separate R3 Outcome, Decision Evaluation, and Lesson

## Context

R3 must close the first coherent human Investment Decision slice with hindsight-safe retrospective semantics. Existing authority already distinguishes Outcome, Decision Evaluation, and Lesson and forbids favorable or unfavorable economic results from automatically determining decision quality.

## Decision

R3 persists Outcome, Decision Evaluation, and Lesson as three separate first-class durable facts with opaque identities.

Outcome is a decision-relative observed consequence. It preserves Investment Decision identity, observed/as-of temporal scope, authoritative source provenance, material consequence dimensions, maturity or partial state, and uncertainty where needed. It does not by itself claim causality or decision quality. Multiple Outcome observations may accumulate through time without rewriting earlier ones.

Decision Evaluation is an attributable, time-specific retrospective judgment preserving target, evaluator Actor Attribution, evaluation time, explicit criteria, the historically faithful judgment-time Decision Context and Evidence basis, later Evidence separately identified as later, relevant Outcome observations, material uncertainty, and causal limitations.

The minimum R3 evaluative dimensions are Evidence/process quality, reasoning/challenge quality, Portfolio/Risk quality, Recommendation quality, Human-decision process where assessable, and Outcome as a separate observed dimension. Ex-ante process quality uses only what was actually available at the historical judgment boundary; ex-post understanding may use later Evidence and Outcome but may not rewrite Judgment-Time Availability.

A favorable Outcome may coexist with weak process. An unfavorable Outcome may coexist with sound process. Evaluation may remain mixed or indeterminate rather than collapse to one universal score.

Lesson is a durable attributable scoped learning proposition formed only when supported by Decision Evaluation and Evidence. It preserves proposition, applicability/scope, source Evaluation(s), supporting Evidence, material uncertainty, formation time, and relationships to prior Lessons when refined, challenged, or superseded. Outcome alone never creates a Lesson automatically.

R3 persists and exposes Lessons but does not yet make them alter future Attention, scheduling, Decision Context, Policy, Investment Mandate, Formal Constraint, or authority. Those effects remain R6 or a separate authority lifecycle.

## Rationale

Keeping observation, retrospective judgment, and learning separate prevents hindsight laundering and result-based quality claims while giving R6 a clean future input.

## Considered Options

A combined retrospective record, P&L-driven evaluation, and generic AI postmortem text were rejected because they collapse established semantics or lose historical knowledge boundaries. Separate durable Outcome, Decision Evaluation, and Lesson facts are accepted.
