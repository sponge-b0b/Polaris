# Resolve future-effective Portfolio Evidence target corrections

## Context

Projected Portfolio Consequence and Portfolio Risk Assessment are Portfolio & Risk-owned Evidence targets with complete target-owned claim catalogs. Accepted Evidence contracts require historical `(T,K)` meaning, current-support owner revisions, and owner-attested temporal cause. They did not determine how a later immediate correction interacts with an already-known future-effective correction on the same root. Different choices would change the target and claim meaning used by Evidence and dependent judgments. This gap blocked Spec #359 ticket #407.

Future-only, unrelated, or display-only Portfolio changes do not stale the exact basis unless they enter a protected dependency. - `context.versions` — portfolio_risk_evidence_context: required invariant — When materially selected for an exact R3 Evidence basis, Portfolio & Risk contributes typed positive root-local revisions for PortfolioBoundaryId, actual PortfolioStateId, ProjectedPortfolioStateId, InvestmentMandateId, and InvestmentStrategyId. A revision begins at 1 and advances once per owner commit changing current interpreted content, applicability, support or provenance, validity, or relationship meaning; a new attributable fact or fresh as-of Portfolio State receives a new root. The complete Mandate revision covers applicable Investment Objective and Investment Principle membership, content, temporal applicability, and exact dependent member references. External source and as-of provenance remain attached to canonical Portfolio State.
- `target.correction-overlap-admission` — portfolio_risk_evidence_target: required invariant — R3 admits future-effective same-root corrections to Projected Portfolio Consequence and Portfolio Risk Assessment targets and their complete target-owned claim catalogs. The target assertion and complete catalog are distinct correction lanes. Each immutable Portfolio & Risk correction act has an opaque owner-issued act reference typed by target family and root, distinct from JudgmentRevision and ClaimCatalogVersion, plus attribution, basis, effective/recorded/known boundaries, affected lane(s), and complete same-root correction ancestry. At atomic admission, an act changing a lane must explicitly resolve every already-known pending future-effective act whose effect would overlap on that lane: supersede/cancel that pending act, or retain it as scheduled successor and expressly bound the new act as interim until the retained act's effective boundary. A multi-lane act resolves overlaps on every affected lane in the same commit. Validate the complete post-commit known effective timeline at all relevant boundaries and the coherent selected target/catalog pair: each lane has a determinate selected or explicit owner-contested interpretation, and a selected catalog must be valid for the selected target assertion. Different lane acts or intervals are permitted only when that pair remains coherent; a one-lane act leaves the other lane's valid meaning intact. Reject unresolved overlap, missing ancestry, incompatible pairing, or implicit precedence without commit. Recorded recency, effective-time recency, UUID/database order, and owner-version order never select a winner.
- `target.correction-overlap-failure` — portfolio_risk_evidence_target: required invariant — At exact read and fresh trusted T=K atomic dependent-write revalidation, validate the complete Portfolio & Risk target/catalog history and prior/fresh owner witnesses. A lawful material same-version target change maps to TARGET_CHANGED; a lawful material claim change maps to CLAIM_CHANGED; report both when independently applicable, and add NEGATIVE_PREDICATE_BROKEN for a broken protected ancestry, membership, selection, or absence guard. Missing, unavailable, not-yet-effective, incomplete, or contested owner meaning returns its applicable distinct no-basis outcome. Contradictory or unexplained same-version meaning or history is INVALID_HISTORY. Invalid/no-basis and stale outcomes commit no dependent judgment, require a fresh read, and are never silently rebased or converted into a new stale category.
- `target.correction-overlap-temporal` — portfolio_risk_evidence_target: required invariant — Reconstruct the target assertion and complete claim-catalog lanes at (effective_at=T, known_at=K) only from acts known by K and effective by T, their explicit ancestry/supersession and interval bounds, never from later knowledge; retain original and defeated acts for inspection. In the A/B/C overlap, A governs before K1; future B recorded at K1 for T3 leaves A current for T<T3, while reads with K1<=K<K2 and T>=T3 select the then-known B. Immediate C recorded at K2<T3 is inadmissible without explicit B resolution on every lane it overlaps. At K>=K2, A governs T<K2 and C governs K2<=T<T3 on affected lanes. If C cancels B, C governs at T>=T3 until another lawful change; if C retains B, C ends at T3 and B governs from T3. At K<K2, later C cannot rewrite the then-known schedule. Apply the same rule to complete catalog membership, proposition meaning, and surviving support, independently by affected lane and subject to coherent target/catalog pairing.
- `target.correction-overlap-version` — portfolio_risk_evidence_target: required invariant — A valid future-only B append at K1 advances neither current JudgmentRevision nor current ClaimCatalogVersion. An atomic C commit at K2 advances JudgmentRevision once iff current target assertion, support/provenance, validity, or applicability changes, and ClaimCatalogVersion once iff current catalog membership, proposition meaning, or surviving support changes; advance both when both change, and neither merely for a row append or pending-schedule change that leaves current meaning/support and history validity intact. Clock activation of retained B at T3 creates no synthetic owner commit or version advance. The owner preserves complete version histories and attests exact (T,K) meaning and temporal cause bound to typed target family/root, correction-act references, both version histories, basis key and read boundary, owner provenance, selected/pending/defeated ancestry, explicit overlap resolution, effective/recorded/known boundaries and intervals, and applicable selection/absence guards. Application validates owner proof and never infers precedence or fabricates a version.
- `targets.versioned` — portfolio_risk_evidence_target: required invariant — Projected Portfolio Consequence and Portfolio Risk Assessment are admitted Evidence judgment target families. Each carries its family-specific typed root identity and a positive root-local JudgmentRevision issued and preserved by Portfolio & Risk. A root starts at revision 1; one atomic owner commit advances it once when that root's current interpreted assertion, support or provenance, validity, or applicability changes. Correction of the same attributable judgment retains the root; a new material judgment, reassessment, or superseding judgment receives a new root at revision 1. Historical (T,K) reads use owner history at that boundary rather than the present-day revision.

## Rationale

Portfolio & Risk must make the relationship between an immediate correction and each known pending act explicit. The chosen rule permits an owner to cancel a pending change or issue a deliberately interim correction, while preserving the knowledge available at each historical cutoff. Separate target and catalog lanes allow a correction to affect only the meaning it changes; admission checks their combined coherence. Version changes describe current support at an owner commit, and the temporal witness explains a later clock activation without inventing a new version.

## Considered Options

Excluding future-effective correction from R3 would reduce implementation work but leave a Portfolio-specific temporal limit. Implicit recorded-time or effective-time precedence would silently choose between owner intentions. Treating every overlap as contested would prevent a deliberate cancellation or interim correction. Explicit overlap resolution adds admission and proof work in exchange for attributable deterministic meaning.
