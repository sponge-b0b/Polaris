# Application Use Cases (Entity ID: application-use-cases)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns commands, queries, cross-entity use-case coordination, application transaction semantics, idempotency, continuity arbitration, concurrency protection, and inward-owned capability ports. It is distinct because interfaces and infrastructure must reach business truth through one application boundary rather than establishing alternate write paths.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* Application commands may establish durable business facts; queries assemble current or historical views without becoming authoritative business writers. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Application use cases own business transaction boundaries, expected-version checks, idempotency, and atomic registration of required durable follow-up where the originating use case requires it. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Long-running model or external calls must not hold durable-store transactions open; state and governing preconditions are re-checked before committing resulting judgments. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Application contracts depend inward on domain semantics and must not import concrete infrastructure or interface implementations. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0001-platform-use-modular-monolith-with-ports-and-adapters.md)

### Planned

* **R2 Investment Decision application contract** — coordinate initiation, partial/complete Scope refinement, Decisions-side human-Deferral and substantive-resolution consequences, work withdrawal/resumption, External Resolution, unsupported-Need correction, renewal, Supersession, lifecycle correction, and Decision Memory queries through technology-neutral application contracts. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md)
* **Explicit and durable continuity arbitration** — initial R2 may inspect all unresolved non-superseded Decisions rather than inventing a semantic matching heuristic; existing candidates require an explicit continue/create-new determination, missing/inconsistent determination remains ambiguous, and create-new commits only after atomic revalidation. When a distinct Decision commits, its initiation history preserves the candidate IDs and attributable basis explaining why a new identity was chosen. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Cross-owner human-judgment seams** — Deferral and substantive resolution require typed trusted Governance-owned Human Investment Decision bases whose semantic effect is explicitly deferring or substantively resolving; R2 exercises only the Decisions-side consequences with deterministic trusted fixtures. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md; docs/proposed/platform-domain-interaction-map.md)
* **Operative-work guard and re-Deferral** — a supportably superseded unresolved Decision cannot receive ordinary resume/defer/resolve/Subject/Scope work; contested Supersession support also fails closed for ordinary work. A later attributable human re-Deferral on an operative deferred Decision appends another Deferral fact without overwriting the first. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Late correction paths** — ordinary External Resolution applies to a currently unresolved Decision, while late External Resolution or unsupported-Need findings that qualify an already recorded disposition enter through append-only correction. Prior human and Polaris acts remain historical. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Lifecycle correction and temporal queries** — append-only corrections preserve earlier recorded facts and distinguish `as_known_at` from effective-at-under-a-knowledge-cutoff; incompatible currently supported interpretations remain contested rather than being selected by write order. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md)
* **Actor/provenance separation** — application preserves who formed/performed a domain act separately from the request/observation trigger and technical model/provider/work correlation. (source: docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md; docs/proposed/platform-domain-interaction-map.md)
* **Decision relationship coordination** — application establishes renewal/Supersession edges and later contextual bindings; the Decision aggregate does not discover graph neighbors. Future `PRIOR_DECISION_CONTEXT` is created only after attributable material use and preserves the target Decision historical state actually used. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md)
