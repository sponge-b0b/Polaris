# Durable Persistence (Entity ID: durable-persistence)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns the durable transactional storage capability needed to preserve direct business truth, immutable history, current-state access, concurrency/continuity protection, temporal correction, and recovery. It is distinct because database products and physical schemas are adapter choices, while persistence guarantees are inward-owned architectural requirements.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* Material business facts are persisted directly under their owning semantics; workflow, job, report, model, or generic runtime event history is not the business source of truth. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)
* Persistence contracts express atomicity, durability, uniqueness, idempotency, historical preservation, concurrency, and reconstruction requirements without exposing PostgreSQL, ORM sessions, SQL expressions, or other adapter-native types. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0003-platform-insulate-infrastructure-behind-inward-owned-capability-ports.md)
* PostgreSQL is the initial/reference 0.2.0 persistence adapter, not the architectural identity of persistence. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0003-platform-insulate-infrastructure-behind-inward-owned-capability-ports.md)
* Greenfield Polaris uses a fresh persistence and migration lineage; current migrations must not target legacy schema objects because they already exist. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)

### Planned

* **R2 Investment Decision persistence design** — use a narrow Decisions command store plus Decision Memory reader rather than a generic repository/UoW framework; atomically preserve current projections, immutable lifecycle facts/corrections, command receipts, and typed relationship facts while keeping PostgreSQL adapter-specific mechanisms inwardly invisible. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Independent lifecycle dimensions** — physical persistence keeps Decision Need status, judgment-resolution status, unresolved work posture, and Supersession separate; External Resolution is persisted as Decision Need elimination and Supersession is a many-to-many relationship rather than a replacement lifecycle status. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Scope completeness** — persistence explicitly represents zero/partial/established Portfolio applicability rather than encoding unresolved Scope through null/sentinel Portfolio identity; material Scope establishment/refinement remains historical. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Continuity-safe initiation** — initial R2 may serialize initiation or use another adapter mechanism to atomically revalidate the full unresolved non-superseded candidate universe; operation idempotency alone is insufficient to prevent semantic duplicate Decisions. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **One Need / one Decision integrity** — every Decision references exactly one grounding Decision Need and one Decision Need grounds at most one Investment Decision; independently resolvable choices receive distinct Needs/Decisions. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Many-to-many Decision relationships** — R2 persistence supports renewal/Supersession without one-to-one constraints; later `PRIOR_DECISION_CONTEXT` can preserve a target knowledge cutoff without redefining the inward contract or requiring a graph database. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md)
* **Dual temporal reconstruction and correction** — append-only corrections preserve original recorded history while current supported effective history may change; `as_known_at` and effective-at-under-knowledge-cutoff are distinct query semantics. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Actor/provenance separation** — persistence must preserve domain Actor Attribution separately from trigger/source and technical request/model/provider/work provenance rather than collapsing them into one generic origin field. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
