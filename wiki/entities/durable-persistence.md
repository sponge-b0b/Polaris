# Durable Persistence (Entity ID: durable-persistence)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns the durable transactional storage capability needed to preserve direct business truth, immutable history, current-state access, concurrency/continuity protection, temporal correction, and recovery. It is distinct because database products and physical schemas are adapter choices, while persistence guarantees are inward-owned architectural requirements.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* Material business facts are persisted directly under their owning semantics; workflow, job, report, model, or generic runtime event history is not the business source of truth. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)
* Persistence contracts express atomicity, durability, uniqueness, idempotency, historical preservation, concurrency, and reconstruction requirements without exposing PostgreSQL, ORM sessions, SQL expressions, or other adapter-native types. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0003-platform-insulate-infrastructure-behind-inward-owned-capability-ports.md)
* PostgreSQL is the initial/reference 0.2.0 persistence adapter, not the architectural identity of persistence. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0003-platform-insulate-infrastructure-behind-inward-owned-capability-ports.md)
* Greenfield Polaris uses a fresh persistence and migration lineage; current migrations must not target legacy schema objects because they already exist. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)

### Planned

* **R2 Investment Decision persistence design** — use a narrow Decisions command store plus Decision Memory reader rather than a generic repository/UoW framework; atomically preserve current projections, immutable lifecycle facts/corrections, command receipts, and typed relationship facts while keeping PostgreSQL adapter-specific mechanisms inwardly invisible. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Foundation contract persistence** — persist the owner-approved inward types rather than invent adapter identities: canonical `PortfolioId`, UUIDv4-backed Decision/Need/Operation identities, unordered unique Scope membership, typed actor attribution, constrained trigger/technical provenance, and fact-specific business bases. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-foundation-public-contract.md)
* **UUID identity persistence** — preserve domain/application UUIDv4-backed `PortfolioId`, `InvestmentDecisionId`, `DecisionNeedId`, and `OperationId` values using native PostgreSQL UUID representation; do not replace them with semantic strings, database-generated integer business identity, or other adapter-defined inward contracts. Database uniqueness/cardinality remains defense in depth. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-foundation-public-contract.md)
* **Need/Decision and Scope constraints** — persistence enforces one Decision Need grounding at most one Investment Decision, preserves unresolved/partial Scope explicitly, treats confirmed Portfolio membership as unordered/unique, and rejects `ESTABLISHED` Scope with zero Portfolio identities. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-foundation-public-contract.md)
* **Lifecycle/work/operative separation** — physical persistence keeps supported lifecycle interpretation, unresolved work posture, and operative applicability/Supersession semantics distinct; a resolved Decision can later be superseded without rewriting its historical disposition, and contested Supersession support remains representable instead of being forced into operative/non-operative. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Continuity-safe and explainable initiation** — R2 may serialize initiation broadly or use another adapter mechanism to atomically revalidate the unresolved operative candidate basis; when a new Decision commits, durable initiation history preserves the continuity determination, materially considered candidate IDs, attributable create-new basis/rationale, and revalidation knowledge/guard reference. Operation idempotency alone is insufficient. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Many-to-many Decision relationships** — R2 persistence supports renewal/Supersession without one-to-one constraints; later `PRIOR_DECISION_CONTEXT` can preserve a target knowledge cutoff/version boundary without redefining the inward contract or requiring a graph database. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md)
* **Dual temporal reconstruction and lifecycle correction** — append-only lifecycle corrections preserve original recorded history while current supported effective history may change; `as_known_at(K)` means state effective at K using only knowledge recorded by K, future-effective known facts do not apply early, late External Resolution or unsupported-Need findings may qualify earlier dispositions, and incompatible supported interpretations remain contested rather than newest-wins. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Reconstructable lifecycle support and append-time knowledge** — preserve non-decreasing recording time with lifecycle-sequence ties resolved by the strict earlier prefix for lifecycle admission. Keep known future-effective lifecycle-correction ancestry available so independently active descendants reconstruct correctly. Projections expose explicit `(T,K)` and surviving whole-result support, distinguish unknown-at-K from `NOT_YET_EFFECTIVE`, and preserve defeated facts separately. Later lifecycle correction changes interpretation without rewriting an act's original validity. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-lifecycle-correction-support-contract.md)
* **Lifecycle correction receipts do not erase equivalent support** — exact replay is the only lifecycle-correction append no-op; each distinct valid act retains a fresh fact ID even when equivalent. Receipt/request conflict handling remains transactional here and in Application. Compare histories at the same recording boundary for current version changes, including support-only changes; future-only append may repeat version, and query or clock passage creates no synthetic commit. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-lifecycle-correction-support-contract.md)
* **Relationship history is reconstructable without physical ordering authority** — every base relationship/correction fact keeps its opaque identity, explicit same-lineage target ancestry, purpose-specific relationship/correction basis roles, and effective/recorded temporal data. `QUALIFY` preserves separate correction and replacement-claim effective instants. Same-command ancestry is an explicit acyclic target-reference graph rather than request-list, insert, UUID, timestamp, or adapter sequence order. Persistence rebuilds exactly `SUPPORTED | CONTESTED | WITHDRAWN | NOT_EFFECTIVE`, complete support IDs, role-associated bases, not-found-at-cutoff, and invalid/incomplete-history outcomes. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md; docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Historical relationship admission remains explainable** — positive relationship facts preserve enough authoritative endpoint/admission history to show why they were valid under knowledge at the trusted recording boundary, including the additional resolved-predecessor constraints for renewal. Later endpoint lifecycle correction never rewrites that historical relationship record; explicit relationship correction owns relationship-semantic change, while current lifecycle/applicability consumers remain independent. Late omitted renewal lineage is append-only against the existing source/Need when historical prerequisites are proven. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md; docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Relationship versions and transactional graph protection are separate** — both source and target `DecisionVersion` protect complete current incident relationship interpretation, including support/basis changes, but future/history-only facts and non-endpoint graph changes can alter cycle/admission safety without a version bump. Persistence therefore enforces expected versions plus authoritative protection/revalidation of material histories, correction ancestry, graph paths, known-future topology and absence predicates through atomic commit. The intentionally higher contention from support-sensitive both-endpoint versions is accepted; adapter coordination may be optimized without weakening semantics. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md; docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md)
* **Actor/provenance separation** — persistence preserves domain Actor Attribution separately from trigger/source and technical request/model/provider/work provenance rather than collapsing them into one generic origin field. (source: docs/proposed/durable-persistence-investment-decision-history.md; docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-foundation-public-contract.md)
