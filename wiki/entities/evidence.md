# Evidence (Entity ID: evidence)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns attributable Evidence identity, source provenance, temporal meaning, fitness for judgment, and the bindings that show which Evidence materially supported a judgment or governed use. It is distinct because Polaris may reason from external facts without taking over the external system's factual authority.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* Decision Context is assembled from canonical domain facts and durable judgment-relative Evidence bindings rather than persisted as a separately authoritative aggregate; historical queries must distinguish what actually applied from what information was available to the particular judgment. (source: docs/adr/0006-evidence-assemble-decision-context-from-durable-evidence-bindings.md)
* Evidence preserves source provenance, observation/as-of time, Judgment-Time Availability, and the information needed to evaluate freshness and support. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* External specialist systems remain authoritative for facts inside their responsibility domains; Polaris records attributable observations and derives decision meaning without silently replacing external factual authority. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Evidence sufficiency, unresolved or missing support, and conflicting Evidence must remain explicit rather than being inferred from model confidence or absence of an error. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* A later change in Evidence fitness may change current support without erasing the historical Evidence observation or the fact that it supported an earlier judgment. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* R3 gives first-class opaque UUIDv4 identity only to independently attributable and reconstructable Evidence observations, judgment bindings, sufficiency assessments, and correction acts. Their identity is independent of content, source, target, chronology, database representation, and workflow identity. (source: docs/adr/0012-evidence-freeze-r3-identity-binding-and-reconstruction.md)
* Observation, binding, and sufficiency-assessment corrections are immutable and append-only under the Evidence-owned `REVISE | RETRACT` vocabulary. Each realized lineage targets only its own root or a prior same-root correction, requires complete acyclic ancestry, recursively restores the preceding branch when a correction is retracted, coalesces equivalent surviving assertions, and preserves incompatible or positive/withdrawal siblings as `CONTESTED`; recency, UUID order, and database order never choose a winner. Assessment replacements retain the original attributable boundary and requirement set/version while deriving their complete proof from the authoritative binding universe. (source: docs/adr/0012-evidence-freeze-r3-identity-binding-and-reconstruction.md; docs/adr/0014-evidence-derive-r3-sufficiency-from-executable-requirements.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/evidence/corrections.py; src/polaris/application/evidence/corrections.py)
* R3 Evidence binds only to the closed accepted judgment-family union with explicit judgment-wide or target-owned claim scope, fixed role/use vocabularies, and historical interpretation at `(effective_at, known_at)`. (source: docs/adr/0012-evidence-freeze-r3-identity-binding-and-reconstruction.md)
* R3 claim-specific Evidence uses target-owned `(EvidenceJudgmentRef, ClaimId)` identity and authoritative versioned claim catalogs; requirement evaluation uses exactly one applicable immutable Configuration-owned requirement-set version; and command-bound current support protects the exact Decision/target/scope/use basis through typed versions, negative predicates, commit-time temporal re-evaluation, and atomic cross-owner validation. (source: docs/adr/0013-evidence-complete-r3-claim-requirements-and-current-support-basis.md)
* R3 Evidence owns the closed executable sufficiency predicate vocabulary and deterministic evaluator. `MinimumEligibleEvidence` admits only a positive distinct-observation minimum and a non-empty subset of `SUPPORTING | CONFLICTING | CONSTRAINING | QUALIFYING`; contextual and reconstruction roles cannot satisfy it, and prose cannot replace predicate identity. At exact `(effective_at, known_at)`, evaluation derives contributors and typed deficiencies from the complete exact-key interpreted binding universe, including surviving observation and binding correction branches and their complete fact support, counts distinct observation identities, preserves each surviving binding assertion and its assessment-time freshness as a typed proof branch, preserves every relied-upon fact reference and exact freshness authority, requires exact negative witnesses, and entails each disposition and aggregate result without caller-authored authority. (source: docs/adr/0014-evidence-derive-r3-sufficiency-from-executable-requirements.md; docs/adr/0012-evidence-freeze-r3-identity-binding-and-reconstruction.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/evidence/sufficiency_requirements.py; src/polaris/domain/evidence/sufficiency.py)
* Historical Decision Context/Evidence reconstruction now interprets complete observation, binding, and assessment correction histories at explicit `(effective_at, known_at)`, preserves each assessment root’s original derived proof and requirement version through later correction/reassessment, and separates judgment-time bindings from retrospective later Evidence. Domain availability, freshness, and sufficiency remain visible as Evidence meaning rather than transport failure. (source: docs/adr/0006-evidence-assemble-decision-context-from-durable-evidence-bindings.md; docs/adr/0012-evidence-freeze-r3-identity-binding-and-reconstruction.md; docs/adr/0014-evidence-derive-r3-sufficiency-from-executable-requirements.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/application/evidence/reconstruction.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/evidence_reconstruction_store.py)
* The greenfield Evidence boundary realizes the closed ten-family owner-specific judgment-reference union, dependent `ClaimId`, exact scope/use vocabulary, and durable `EvidenceBinding` roots with fixed observation/target/scope/use endpoints, the accepted role and availability vocabularies, material-use gating, optional material qualification, and complete typed freshness outcomes. Applicable freshness preserves the exact Configuration set/version/requirement and evaluation basis with exactly `FRESH | STALE | INDETERMINATE`; resolved no-requirement applicability preserves an exact set/version negative witness; and missing, unavailable, contested, and invalid authority remain distinct. Target owners embed complete positive `ClaimCatalogVersion` histories whose operations retain identity only for same-proposition correction, require fresh IDs for material changes and new roots, and preserve retracted historical membership. Application resolves claim membership and freshness authority at `(effective_at, known_at)` through inward contracts before binding admission. Independently attributable duplicate endpoint tuples remain legal.
