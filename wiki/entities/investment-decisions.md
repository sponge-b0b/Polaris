# Investment Decisions (Entity ID: investment-decisions)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns first-class Investment Decision identity and lifecycle continuity: Decision Need, Subject and Decision Scope relationships, unresolved/resumable work, Decisions-side consequences of Deferral and substantive resolution, External Resolution, correction of unsupported Decision Need/lifecycle understanding, Supersession relationships, and causally linked renewed decisions. The boundary is distinct because later Evidence, Recommendations, authority acts, Action Intents, Outcomes, and technical runtime activity must reference decision identity without being allowed to redefine it.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* An Investment Decision represents one coherent unresolved Portfolio-relevant choice; a resolved decision never reopens, and renewed judgment after resolution creates a new causally linked Investment Decision. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Deferral preserves the same unresolved Investment Decision, while new Evidence, state, Risk, or Recommendation change does not by itself create a new decision. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Investment Decision identity must remain independent of workflow, job, model invocation, report/output, and other technical execution identity because technical provenance is not business identity. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)
* Investment Decision is a lifecycle root referenced by other semantic owners, not a giant aggregate that absorbs Evidence, judgment, authority, Action Intent, Outcome, or Lesson ownership. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Durable historical truth is preserved directly under business semantics and later change is represented explicitly rather than silently rewriting earlier decision facts. (source: docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)

### Planned

* **R2 decision kernel and historical truth** — establish the smallest durable implementation of Investment Decision identity, historical reconstruction, transaction/idempotency/concurrency semantics, and technology-neutral persistence while keeping later domain owners out of the aggregate. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-decision-kernel-component-boundaries.md)
* **Multi-dimensional lifecycle design** — separate resolution disposition (`UNRESOLVED`, substantive resolution, External Resolution, unsupported-Need retraction), unresolved work disposition (`ACTIVE`, human-`DEFERRED`, `WITHDRAWN`), and Supersession as an orthogonal relationship. Decision Scope may remain explicitly unresolved during initiation; Deferral requires a trusted Human Investment Decision basis; actor attribution remains separate from trigger/technical provenance. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Non-destructive temporal correction** — preserve effective time separately from recorded/known time; support both `as_known_at` and effective-at-under-knowledge-cutoff reconstruction; append explicit lifecycle corrections rather than deleting earlier facts when late information changes the supported effective interpretation. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Continuity arbitration** — distinct concurrent initiation operations must revalidate bounded unresolved-candidate continuity before committing a new Decision; ambiguity fails closed rather than silently manufacturing duplicate Decision identity. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md; docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md)
* **Decision relationship model** — represent `RENEWED_FROM` and `SUPERSEDES` as typed durable graph edges; Supersession may target unresolved or resolved Decisions without rewriting historical resolution and has no assumed one-to-one cardinality. Later `PRIOR_DECISION_CONTEXT` binds the target Decision state as known when materially used; candidate retrieval alone never creates that binding. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md)
