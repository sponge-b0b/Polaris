# Investment Decisions (Entity ID: investment-decisions)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns first-class Investment Decision identity and lifecycle continuity: Decision Need, Decision Subject and Scope relationships, unresolved/resumable work, Decisions-side consequences of human Deferral and substantive resolution, External Resolution, correction of unsupported Decision Need/lifecycle understanding, Supersession relationships, and causally linked renewed Decisions. The boundary is distinct because later Evidence, Recommendations, authority acts, Action Intents, Outcomes, and runtime activity must reference Decision identity without redefining it.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* An Investment Decision represents one coherent unresolved Portfolio-relevant choice; a resolved Decision never reopens, and renewed judgment after resolution creates a new causally linked Investment Decision. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Deferral preserves the same unresolved Investment Decision, while new Evidence, state, Risk, or Recommendation change does not by itself create a new Decision. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Investment Decision identity remains independent of workflow, job, model invocation, report/output, and other technical execution identity. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)
* Investment Decision is a lifecycle root referenced by other semantic owners, not a giant aggregate that absorbs Evidence, judgment, authority, Action Intent, Outcome, or Lesson ownership. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Durable historical truth is preserved directly under business semantics and later change is represented explicitly rather than silently rewriting earlier Decision facts. (source: docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)

### Planned

* **R2 decision kernel and historical truth** — establish the smallest durable implementation of Investment Decision identity, historical reconstruction, transaction/idempotency/concurrency semantics, and technology-neutral persistence while keeping later domain owners out of the Decision aggregate. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-decision-kernel-component-boundaries.md)
* **Need/Decision identity cardinality** — every Investment Decision is grounded in exactly one Decision Need and one Need grounds at most one Decision; continuation of an existing unresolved choice does not manufacture another Need, while genuinely new or renewed judgment uses a new Need. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Lifecycle/work/applicability separation** — preserve supported lifecycle disposition (`UNRESOLVED`, `SUBSTANTIVELY_RESOLVED`, `EXTERNALLY_RESOLVED`, `NEED_RETRACTED_UNSUPPORTED`), unresolved work posture (`ACTIVE`, human-`DEFERRED`, `WITHDRAWN`), and Supersession as distinct semantics rather than one status enum. Supersession changes continuing applicability without rewriting historical lifecycle disposition. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Scope completeness** — Decision Scope may begin with zero or some confirmed Portfolios while completeness remains unresolved; `ESTABLISHED` Scope requires at least one Portfolio. Later establishment/refinement preserves history and must not use a fake/default Portfolio identity. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Human-Deferral and operative-work semantics** — Deferral requires a trusted Human Investment Decision basis; a later attributable re-Deferral appends another Deferral fact; deliberate hold/no-action may substantively resolve while Recommendation rejection requesting more judgment does not; and ordinary unresolved work/judgment consequences require determinately operative applicability, failing closed when Supersession support is contested. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Non-destructive lifecycle correction** — unsupported Need or late lifecycle facts may correct any prior supported lifecycle interpretation through append-only facts; prior acts remain historical, `as_known_at(K)` means state effective at K using only knowledge recorded by K, and incompatible supported interpretations remain contested rather than newest-wins. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Explicit and durable continuity arbitration** — initial R2 considers conservative unresolved non-superseded candidates; existing candidates require an explicit continue/create-new determination, missing/inconsistent determination remains ambiguous, create-new commits only after atomic revalidation, and the initiation fact preserves the candidate set and attributable basis explaining why a distinct identity was created. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md; docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md)
* **Decision relationship model** — `RENEWED_FROM` and `SUPERSEDES` are typed durable graph edges; Supersession may target unresolved or resolved Decisions without rewriting lifecycle disposition and has no assumed one-to-one cardinality. Later `PRIOR_DECISION_CONTEXT` binds the target Decision as known when materially used rather than its later current state. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md)
