# Investment Decisions (Entity ID: investment-decisions)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns first-class Investment Decision identity and lifecycle continuity: Decision Need, Decision Subject and Scope relationships, unresolved/resumable work, Decisions-side consequences of human Deferral and substantive resolution, External Resolution as Decision Need elimination, correction of unsupported Decision Need/lifecycle understanding, Supersession relationships, and causally linked renewed Decisions. The boundary is distinct because later Evidence, Recommendations, authority acts, Action Intents, Outcomes, and runtime activity must reference Decision identity without redefining it.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* An Investment Decision represents one coherent unresolved Portfolio-relevant choice; a resolved decision never reopens, and renewed judgment after resolution creates a new causally linked Investment Decision. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Deferral preserves the same unresolved Investment Decision, while new Evidence, state, Risk, or Recommendation change does not by itself create a new Decision. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Investment Decision identity remains independent of workflow, job, model invocation, report/output, and other technical execution identity. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md; docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)
* Investment Decision is a lifecycle root referenced by other semantic owners, not a giant aggregate that absorbs Evidence, judgment, authority, Action Intent, Outcome, or Lesson ownership. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Durable historical truth is preserved directly under business semantics and later change is represented explicitly rather than silently rewriting earlier Decision facts. (source: docs/adr/0002-platform-persist-direct-business-truth-with-immutable-history.md)

### Planned

* **R2 decision kernel and historical truth** — establish the smallest durable implementation of Investment Decision identity, historical reconstruction, transaction/idempotency/concurrency semantics, and technology-neutral persistence while keeping later domain owners out of the Decision aggregate. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-decision-kernel-component-boundaries.md)
* **Four-dimensional lifecycle design** — preserve Decision Need status (`ACTIVE`, externally eliminated, unsupported/retracted), substantive judgment status (`UNRESOLVED` / substantively resolved), unresolved work posture (`ACTIVE`, human-`DEFERRED`, `WITHDRAWN`), and Supersession as independent semantics rather than one status enum. External Resolution changes the Decision Need axis, while Supersession changes continuing applicability without rewriting judgment history. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Scope completeness** — Decision Scope may begin with zero or some confirmed Portfolios while completeness remains unresolved; later establishment/refinement preserves history and must not use a fake/default Portfolio identity. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Human-Deferral and work semantics** — Deferral requires a trusted Human Investment Decision basis; a later human Deferral may re-defer an already deferred Decision without overwriting prior Deferral history, while work withdrawal remains distinct from human judgment and may later resume the same active Need. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Non-destructive Decision Need/lifecycle correction** — an erroneous/unsupported Decision Need may be retracted even after other historical acts occurred; those acts remain historical and any changed lifecycle interpretation is expressed through append-only correction. `as_known_at` and effective-at-under-a-knowledge-cutoff remain distinct. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md)
* **Continuity arbitration** — initial R2 continuity may conservatively consider all unresolved non-superseded Decisions; a distinct new Decision commits only after atomic revalidation, and ambiguity or a changed candidate universe fails closed rather than creating duplicate identity. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-lifecycle-model.md; docs/proposed/application-use-cases-investment-decision-lifecycle.md)
* **Decision relationship model** — `RENEWED_FROM` and `SUPERSEDES` are typed durable graph edges; Supersession may target unresolved or resolved Decisions without rewriting lifecycle axes and has no assumed one-to-one cardinality. Later `PRIOR_DECISION_CONTEXT` binds the target Decision as known when materially used rather than its later current state. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-decision-relationship-model.md)
