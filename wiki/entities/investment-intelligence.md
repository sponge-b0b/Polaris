# Investment Intelligence (Entity ID: investment-intelligence)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns attributable analytical investment judgment: hypotheses, views, theses where applicable, assumptions, uncertainty, invalidation conditions, catalysts, decision alternatives, materially used Signals, Investment Recommendations, and Proposed Actions. It is distinct because analytical judgment must remain separate from human decision authority, deterministic rule results, and external factual truth.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* The first implemented R3 slice binds the accepted instrument identity to SPY and keeps one explicit horizon across a Decision's judgments. It persists attributable, time-specific Investment Views with material Evidence-binding references, assumptions, uncertainty, dissent or alternative explanation, invalidation conditions, and materially used Signal provenance. Hypotheses persist only with a material View or challenge link; durable challenge results require a substantive competing interpretation, challenged assumption/uncertainty/condition, material disposition basis, and the closed `INCONCLUSIVE | QUALIFIED | REJECTED | SURVIVES_CHALLENGE` vocabulary. Material Decision Alternatives retain distinct increase, hold/no-action, and reduce choices with append-only root-local revisions whose economic choice cannot change, plus a guarded current selection. Model/provider and workflow identities remain outside these business facts. (source: docs/adr/0007-investment-intelligence-persist-material-judgments-not-reasoning-traces.md; docs/adr/0011-platform-compose-r3-decision-memory-from-domain-owned-facts.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/investment_intelligence.py; src/polaris/application/investment_intelligence.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/investment_intelligence.py; migrations/versions/0009_investment_intelligence_judgments.py)
* Each View materially relied upon by a consequential Recommendation has its own durable substantive Challenge result before the Recommendation commits. The commit checks the stored Challenge-to-View links for complete coverage of the relied-upon Views. (source: docs/adr/0007-investment-intelligence-persist-material-judgments-not-reasoning-traces.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/investment_intelligence.py; tests/integration/persistence/postgresql/test_recommendation_formation.py)
* Investment Intelligence owns immutable same-root correction acts for its five Evidence judgment targets. Each act carries complete owner ancestry, separate target and claim-catalog lanes, explicit pending future-overlap resolution, and exact `(effective_at, known_at)` interpretation. Current target and catalog versions advance only for changed current meaning at commit; clock activation changes temporal meaning without issuing a version. The owner attests complete temporal cause for dependent Evidence reads. (source: docs/adr/0013-evidence-complete-r3-claim-requirements-and-current-support-basis.md; docs/adr/0015-evidence-complete-r3-target-and-context-version-basis.md; docs/adr/0017-investment-intelligence-resolve-overlapping-future-corrections.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/intelligence_judgment_history.py; src/polaris/application/intelligence_judgment_history.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/intelligence_judgment_history.py)
* Recommendations preserve the material View, challenge, Alternative, Evidence, actual Portfolio State, projected consequence, Risk, uncertainty, and decision-horizon basis of an attributable preference. Explicit withholding is a separate durable judgment from no attempt and from a hold Recommendation. When withholding formation consumes a current Evidence result, its exact basis is preserved and atomically revalidated before commit; stale or unavailable support prevents the write. Preference history is append-only; support is derived from the latest judgment and the currently supplied Decision, Evidence, and Risk fitness, so loss of support never reactivates an older Recommendation. (source: docs/adr/0007-investment-intelligence-persist-material-judgments-not-reasoning-traces.md; docs/adr/0008-portfolio-risk-ground-r3-recommendations-in-projected-consequences.md; docs/adr/0013-evidence-complete-r3-claim-requirements-and-current-support-basis.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/investment_recommendation.py; src/polaris/application/investment_recommendation.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/investment_intelligence.py)
* A current DecisionVersion alone does not admit a new Investment View, challenge, Alternative, or withholding judgment. Their PostgreSQL commit requires a determinately unresolved, operative governing Decision, checked under the same Decision row lock as version validation and reconstructed from known lifecycle and relationship history at commit time. (source: contracts/ccl/transactions/250672ecda3bc8a4bc15500f0c203409b18acdf813bf2cb8ba2f87341ea643d7.json; implementation evidence: src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/investment_intelligence.py; tests/integration/persistence/postgresql/test_investment_intelligence_store.py)
* R3 durable Investment Intelligence preserves material business-semantic judgments rather than reasoning traces: materially relied-upon Investment Views, substantive meaningful-challenge results, material Decision Alternatives, Investment Recommendations, and explicit withholding judgments are durable and append-only, while prompts/model traces/transient brainstorming remain technical or transient. (source: docs/adr/0007-investment-intelligence-persist-material-judgments-not-reasoning-traces.md)
* Investment Intelligence may form and challenge investment judgment, but it does not own Human Investment Decision, Approval, execution authority, or external factual truth. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* AI/model output is a draft analytical result with technical provenance; deterministic validation and application/domain acceptance are required before it becomes an attributable business judgment. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* A model cannot establish Approval, Human Investment Decision, Mandate Exception, Residual-Risk Acceptance, or an external fact by asserting one in generated content. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Meaningful challenge is a product requirement, but no Bull/Bear/Sideways, multi-model, debate-round, agent-registry, or workflow-graph topology is architecturally mandatory. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
