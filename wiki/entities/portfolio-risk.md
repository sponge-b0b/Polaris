# Portfolio & Risk (Entity ID: portfolio-risk)

**Boundary Rationale:** This boundary owns the decision meaning of Portfolio identity and state, Position, Exposure, Allocation, Projected Portfolio State, Projected Portfolio Consequence, Portfolio Risk, Portfolio Risk Assessment, Investment Objectives, Principles, Strategy, Horizon, Mandate, and machine-evaluable Formal Constraints. It is distinct because portfolio consequence and economic risk must remain separate from governance authority and from external books-and-records ownership.
(source: owner-approved entity boundary determination)

### Strict Invariants

* The first R3 use of actual Portfolio State is SPY-scoped. The immutable State has typed instrument, Portfolio, and State identities, explicit as-of and source provenance, capital basis, cash, Position quantity/value, distinct Exposure and Allocation measures, interpretation basis, and retained uncertainty. Its source remains externally authoritative; Polaris owns the decision interpretation. Historical selection is by exact State identity and knowledge boundary, not by overwriting a current snapshot. (source: docs/adr/0008-portfolio-risk-ground-r3-recommendations-in-projected-consequences.md; docs/adr/0011-platform-compose-r3-decision-memory-from-domain-owned-facts.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/portfolio.py; src/polaris/application/portfolio.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/portfolio_states.py)
* Portfolio & Risk records immutable hypothetical Projected Portfolio Consequences for every member of the material Decision Alternative set read from Investment Intelligence. Each fact retains the typed Alternative root/revision, exact actual State identity and as-of, explicit horizon and scenario assumptions, SPY Exposure or Allocation change, material cash effect when present, benefits, adverse possibilities, Risk changes, Actor Attribution, and explicit uncertainty or unresolved deltas. A full simulated Portfolio State is not required. Investment Intelligence owns the material Alternative set. (source: docs/adr/0008-portfolio-risk-ground-r3-recommendations-in-projected-consequences.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/portfolio_consequences.py; src/polaris/application/portfolio_consequences.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/portfolio_consequences.py)
* R3 Portfolio Risk Assessments are attributable judgments on one exact actual State or projected consequence, preserving horizon, scenario, support basis, typed materially used Evidence references, assumptions, uncertainty, and separate Exposure concentration, downside, liquidity, and other material dimensions. Unsupported downside or projected Exposure remains unresolved instead of becoming a score or fabricated number. Assessments and their source references are immutable historical facts; typed results are available to Recommendation formation without granting Risk approval authority. (source: docs/adr/0008-portfolio-risk-ground-r3-recommendations-in-projected-consequences.md; docs/adr/0011-platform-compose-r3-decision-memory-from-domain-owned-facts.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/portfolio_risk.py; src/polaris/application/portfolio_risk.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/portfolio_risk.py)
* Projected Portfolio Consequences and Portfolio Risk Assessments own complete versioned claim catalogs and append-only correction acts for their typed Evidence judgment roots. Target assertions and catalogs advance separate current versions only when current meaning changes; future acts and clock activation preserve those versions. A later overlapping act explicitly cancels or retains each pending same-lane act, and retained successors bound interim meaning. Exact `(effective_at, known_at)` selection uses complete owner ancestry and coherent target/catalog pairs rather than row order. Application exposes owner-attested target, claim, and temporal-cause reads to Evidence. (source: docs/adr/0013-evidence-complete-r3-claim-requirements-and-current-support-basis.md; docs/adr/0019-portfolio-risk-resolve-future-effective-target-corrections.md; implementation evidence: src/polaris/domain/portfolio_judgment_history.py; src/polaris/application/portfolio_judgment_history.py; src/polaris/infrastructure/persistence/postgresql/portfolio_judgment_history.py)
* R3 Recommendation formation is Portfolio-grounded: decision-grade actual Portfolio State, material alternative-relative Projected Portfolio Consequences, and attributable multidimensional Portfolio Risk Assessments are durable inputs before Recommendation finality; Risk is not a post-hoc approval or authority fact. (source: docs/adr/0008-portfolio-risk-ground-r3-recommendations-in-projected-consequences.md)
* Portfolio identity is a continuing investment responsibility rather than merely a current set of holdings, and Position, Exposure, Allocation, and Portfolio Risk remain distinct concepts. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Authoritative operational Portfolio State may come from an external specialist source; Polaris owns its decision meaning and derived consequences without claiming external books-and-records authority. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Projected Portfolio Consequence and Portfolio Risk are economic decision inputs, not approval or authority facts. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)
* Machine-evaluable Formal Constraints are deterministic rules; their evaluation remains distinct from power-specific human authority acts. (source: docs/current/platform-architecture-0.2.0.md)

### Planned

* **Canonical Portfolio domain identity** — `Portfolio` is the durable domain entity/concept owned by Portfolio & Risk, while this wiki page is the architectural ownership boundary. `PortfolioId` is the canonical immutable UUIDv4-backed opaque identity of that domain entity, allocated independently of Portfolio contents/state and carrying no business semantics. Other boundaries reference `PortfolioId` rather than inventing local Portfolio identity types; a full Portfolio aggregate implementation is not required merely to establish the identity contract. (source: docs/proposed/investment-decisions-r2-foundation-public-contract.md)
* **Action-continuity reconciliation seam** — authoritative external activity may change Portfolio State while Action Continuity separately owns intended-vs-observed association/reconciliation. One Portfolio change may externally eliminate one Decision Need, materially alter another unresolved Decision, and contribute to a different Decision Need; Portfolio & Risk owns the resulting Portfolio meaning without manufacturing Action Intent causality or Decision lifecycle meaning. (source: docs/proposed/platform-domain-interaction-map.md)
